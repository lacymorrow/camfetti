Privacy

Last updated October 31, 2026

CamFetti doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising in the app.

Your cameras

The camera list stays on your iPhone or Mac. Passwords and API keys are kept in that device's Keychain and never leave it. Your Apple Watch gets camera names and pictures from your iPhone, never passwords. The app connects only to the camera addresses you add, plus the addresses it finds while scanning your local network for cameras.

Local network

iOS asks for local network permission so CamFetti can find cameras on your Wi-Fi and play their video. Nothing about your network is sent anywhere.

Photos

When you tap the snapshot button, the picture is saved to your photo library. CamFetti asks only for permission to add photos, so it can't read your library.

Tips

Tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Video

Video goes directly from your camera to your phone. It doesn't pass through any server we run.
