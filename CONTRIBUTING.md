# Contributing

Open an issue for bugs, and include your iPhone model, iOS version and camera model. Pull requests are welcome, and small ones get reviewed fastest.

Before you open one, run the tests with the fake cameras described in the README.

## Contributor agreement

The first time you contribute, add a comment to your pull request saying "I agree to the CLA". [CLA.md](CLA.md) is short. You keep the copyright to your work, and it lets the project ship your code, including on the App Store, where the GPL alone isn't enough.
